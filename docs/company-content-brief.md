## Claims requiring supporting evidence

- OEKO-TEX: B1 references an OEKO-TEX standard, but no certificate number, scope or validity dates are supplied. Do not add a certification badge or claim current certification from this alone.
- Wonder Dry: B1 calls the brand patented. The supplied documents do not establish the legal status of a patent or trademark. Brand mention and IP claims are separate matters.
- Extreme temperatures, flame retardancy, sterilisation and wash-cycle counts: B1–B3 contain claims, but no construction-specific test reports or methods are included. Do not publish blanket guarantees or safety assurances.
- Environmental comparisons: B1/B3 contain broad comparisons and superlatives. Use narrowly described process facts instead of claims such as 'most eco-friendly', universally non-toxic, or quantified energy savings.
- Membrane explanations: B3/B4 contain technical statements about pore size, windproofness, curing and thickness-dependent moisture transport that require specialist review. Do not copy these explanations verbatim as educational content.
