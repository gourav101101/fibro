## Current visual direction

The client preferred the richer homepage. Restored three selectable hero stories,
photographic material cards, the separate interactive technology section, the
large material-study/process layout, and the full About story, vision, mission
and values. Retained clear service headings, concise copy, all public-page links,
responsibility logos, tagline and approved logo sizing. Native scrolling and
viewport-aware desktop sections remain. The compact homepage experiment is no
longer in use.

The subsequent refinement keeps this richer composition. Service cards use their
actual service names as headings, one explanatory sentence, aligned links and
subtle hover/focus feedback. Application selection has a clear tinted active
state. Material detail controls align and open one panel at a time. Homepage
Vision and Mission use native expandable panels; the dedicated About page keeps
both descriptions fully visible. Imagery, three hero stories, the interactive
layer diagram, process and all three values remain in place.
